The Non-Negotiables
I

Nothing off-plan — including drinks. No soda, juice, creamer, or alcohol. Water, black coffee, plain tea, and zero-calorie drinks only.

II

Weigh in every morning. After the bathroom, before food or water. Log it. We judge the weekly average, never a single day.

III

Run it 3 full weeks before judging. The first week the scale can do anything. Weeks two and three are where the data lives.

IV

If it's not on this page, it doesn't go in your mouth. If something comes up, text Kyle before you eat it — not after.

Hunger Flex

If hunger is rough the first week: drop the peanut butter and add 1 cup egg whites or a second Greek yogurt. Fewer calories, more protein, more volume.
